AI's Institutional Moment

Why U.S. AI Week. Why Now.

Five years after the launch of generative AI, artificial intelligence is transitioning from technological breakthrough to economic infrastructure. Across the United States, enterprises are deploying AI at scale, researchers are advancing the frontier, policymakers are shaping governance frameworks, and workforce institutions are responding to profound shifts in the nature of work.


Yet much of this progress is happening in parallel silos.

U.S. AI Week was created to bridge this fragmentation — convening leaders across sectors to foster meaningful dialogue, strategic partnerships, and coordinated action to strengthen America's leadership in the global AI economy.
